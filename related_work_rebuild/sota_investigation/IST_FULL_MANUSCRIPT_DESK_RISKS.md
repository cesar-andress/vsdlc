## Editorial decision (whole manuscript)

**Likely desk outcome today: Return / reject without review**, unless a **minimum repositioning + methods honesty pass** is completed first.

Why not “send and let reviewers decide”?

Because the manuscript contains at least three issues that burn referee time without a fair scientific contest:

1. **Framing mismatch:** title/abstract/intro sell “reporting for AI-instruction artifacts”; the only IST-grade payload is **measurement sensitivity of discovery-frame prevalence**.  
2. **Label-process instability is severe and underplayed:** Human~2 vs functional inspection agreement is **24%** (κ −0.164); two inspectors agree **50%**. That threatens the construct before any “contamination rate” can be trusted.  
3. **LLM tie-breaking shapes the headline estimand** (74 human ties resolved by the model), while the paper still markets three-coder consensus as a stable audit outcome.

A rebuilt Related Work cannot rescue a paper whose front matter sells the wrong product and whose annotation reliability story is editorially unfinished.

## Minimum change set that maximizes acceptance probability

Not a full rewrite. The **smallest package** with highest expected lift:

### M1. Reposition to B in title, abstract, intro contributions, conclusion (Effort ~4)
- Title → discovery-frame validity / predicate-based sampling audit (case in subtitle optional).  
- Abstract lead: estimand dependence (protocol + target + family).  
- Contributions: (C1) empirical sensitivity/structure findings; (C2) audit protocol; (C3) reporting implications derived.  
- AI-instruction predicates = **case selection**, one paragraph of criteria.  
- Kill “reporting guidance” as primary claim.

### M2. Make human-only (or dual) estimands primary; demote LLM plurality (Effort ~5)
- Headline RQ1 rates: **human-only protocol** + plurality as sensitivity.  
- Or re-run consensus without LLM voter for primary tables.  
- Explicitly state: LLM is a sensitivity instrument, not authority.  
This directly attacks weakness #3 and reduces ethics/methods hostility.

### M3. Reinterpret RQ4 as a scientific finding about boundary hardness—or repair inspection (Effort ~4–7)
**Minimum path (lower effort):**  
- Elevate inspector disagreement and Human~2 failure as evidence that **membership-boundary labels are fragile**, therefore protocol sensitivity is not a nuisance but expected.  
- Narrow claims: do not treat inspection as gold standard prevalence; treat it as stress test.  
**Stronger path (higher effort):**  
- Adjudication panel for inspection disagreements; drop Human~2 or retrain/recode; report only adjudicated subsample.

Without M3, many IST reviewers will reject on construct grounds regardless of framing.

### M4. Motivation repair in ≤15 lines (Effort ~3)
Either:  
- cite concrete studies that use path/instruction-like discovery, **or**  
- dated negative search + argue anticipatory methodology for an emerging predicate class.  
Remove product-doc spine from sentence 1. Remove self-Zenodo as gap proof.

### M5. One comparison paragraph/table vs RepoReapers/PHANTOM (Effort ~3–6)
Even qualitative: engineered filter ≠ target-conditional role membership; optionally quantify residual off-target after engineered-like rules (you already apply engineering filters—state residual contamination **after** them as the point).

### M6. Align Discussion/Threats/Conclusion to M1–M3 (Effort ~3)
- Discussion: two parts—(i) methodological implications for MSR discovery frames; (ii) case observations.  
- Explicitly discuss Human~2/inspector shock as limits on metadata-only audits.  
- Conclusion: state B claim in one paragraph; no guidance-first ending.

### Explicitly **not** in the minimum set
- Expanding to many frames (helpful later, not minimum).  
- Cutting all RW length (nice, not decisive).  
- More ML baselines (likely hurts).  
- More Copilot/promptware literature (pulls back to A).
