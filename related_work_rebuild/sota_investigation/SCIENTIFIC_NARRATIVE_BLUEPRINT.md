## The novelty that already exists (name it once, then protect it)

Not: GitHub is noisy.  
Not: files named `AGENTS.md` exist.  
Not: reporting checklists or replication packages.

**Yes:**

1. **Estimand dependence** — On the same labeled repositories, contamination prevalence moves under alternative consensus protocols (and under target collapse). Prevalence is therefore a *protocol-defined estimand*, not a frame constant.  
2. **Predicate-family structure** — Misalignment is not uniform; family rankings are more durable than frame-wide rates.  
3. **Boundary hardness** — Disagreement concentrates at membership boundaries more than at product-role distinctions.  
4. **Residual vs engineered curation** — Engineered/activity filters do not discharge target-conditional membership for predicate-based frames (to be shown or stated as the open residual the audit addresses).

Those four are the scientific payload. Guidance and package are *consequences*.

## Narrative discipline rules (for any future rewrite)

1. **Filename rule:** A concrete instruction filename may appear in Case/Method; it may not open Title, Abstract, Intro, or Conclusion.  
2. **Contribution rule:** If a sentence could be true without the Results, it is not C1.  
3. **Citation rule:** Grey docs justify predicates; they never justify “the field already does this.”  
4. **False-friend rule:** Every mention of engineered curation must end in residual, not equivalence.  
5. **Contamination rule:** Always “target-conditional sample–target mismatch,” never “MSR convention.”  
6. **Generalization rule:** “In this case frame…” before any rate; “for predicate-based frames…” only for audit implications.  
7. **Package rule:** Cite Zenodo in Data Availability; never as a co-equal scientific contribution.  
8. **Honesty rule:** Single-frame scope early; joint gap hedged to reviewed clusters; no “increasingly” without evidence.
